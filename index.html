scan

      
Eight factors. One percent. Not a buy signal — a read of the tape GMGN already surfaces on Robinhood.

      

        

          
tape

          
24h / 1h / 5m volume. Quiet books stay muted even if the candle looks tall.

        

        

          
book

          
Pool depth on Uniswap. Thin liquidity is an exit tax, scored as such.

        

        

          
flow

          
Buy vs sell prints. HUNTER marks when the tape is one-sided.

        

        

          
impulse

          
5m and 1h percent. Spikes without volume do not get a free SING.

        

        

          
crowd

          
Holder count and top-10 concentration when Blockscout answers.

        

        

          
fresh

          
Age sweet-spot: new enough to matter, not so new it is still empty.

        

        

          
structure

          
Liquidity versus market cap. Huge mcap on a puddle is exit risk.

        

        

          
x-ray

          
Twitter handle heat when DexScreener has a link. Silence is a score, too.
